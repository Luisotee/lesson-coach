What students often get wrong

Moves a number across without changing its sign

3x + 5 = 20 → 3x = 25
Ask: What did you do to both sides to get 25?

Divides only one term by 3

3x + 5 = 20 → x + 5 = 20 ÷ 3
Ask: If you divide the left side by 3, what happens to the 5?
