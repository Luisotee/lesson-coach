Solving linear equations

An equation says two amounts are equal. Solving it means finding the number that keeps them equal, by doing the same thing to both sides.

On this page The idea Refresh first Worked example Common mistakes Questions students ask Lesson outline

The idea

Think of a balance. Both sides weigh the same. Whatever you do to one side, you do to the other, and it stays balanced.

Why it works

Adding, subtracting, multiplying or dividing both sides by the same number (not zero) never changes which x makes them equal. So each step gives a simpler equation with the same answer.
